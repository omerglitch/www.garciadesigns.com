Join artists Omer Garcia @omerglitch every Monday night as they sketch, talk comics, and dive into the art world live! Inspired by the amazing Monday Night Draw hosted by David Finch (with whom we have no affiliation—we’re just massive fans!), our show aims to keep the creative energy alive while Mr. Finch’s stream is on hiatus. Expect a casual, creative hangout filled with fan art, comic book chatter, and behind-the-scenes looks at our artistic process. Whether you’re an artist, a comic book fan, or just love watching creativity in motion, you’re welcome here!

Grab your sketchbook and draw along with us! We encourage viewers to join the conversation, ask questions, and study the art of comics together. Whether you’re learning anatomy, inking techniques, or just want to improve your storytelling, this is a space to grow, learn, and share your passion with a like-minded community.

I am deeply passionate about creating and discussing art, with a particular focus on comics. As an artist, I spend my time drawing and experimenting with various styles, techniques, and mediums, always striving to push the boundaries of my craft. In addition to my own artistic work, I love engaging in thoughtful conversations about art and comics. I explore different perspectives, analyze trends, and reflect on the impact of visual storytelling.

To enrich these discussions, I invite guests who share similar interests and creative ideas to join me on this journey. These guests may include fellow artists, comic creators, critics, or industry professionals who can offer unique insights and provoke new ideas. Whether it’s about the art-making process, storytelling techniques, or the broader cultural significance of comics, these conversations aim to inspire, educate, and spark creativity in anyone who shares a passion for the world of art and comics.
