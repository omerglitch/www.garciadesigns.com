🎨 About @omerglitch – Art, Design, and Storytelling in Motion

Omer Garcia (@omerglitch) is a visual storyteller, comic book artist, and designer specializing in original art, commissions, and creative media production. His work blends bold traditional techniques with modern flair — from ink and marker sketch cards to large-format illustrations and mixed-media paintings.

Through Garcia Designs, Omer provides a range of services and products for collectors, fans, and creative collaborators:

🛠️ Services

  • ✏️ Custom Art Commissions: Original character illustrations, fan art, and comic-style portraits.
  • 📚 Comic & Concept Art: Character design, storyboarding, and sequential art for indie and commercial projects.
  • 🎨 Live Art Streams & Appearances: Interactive drawing shows, collaborations, and community art events.
  • 🖥️ Design Work: Branding, logos, and visual identity design for creative brands and indie projects.

🛍️ Products

  • 🎴 Original Sketch Cards & Prints: Hand-drawn collectibles featuring pop culture, comics, and original creations like Glitch.
  • 🖼️ Limited Edition Art Prints: High-quality signed prints of featured artworks.
  • 📦 Merch & Exclusive Drops: Stickers, posters, and creative collaborations with fellow indie artists.

Every piece Omer creates — from a quick sketch to a finished illustration — is infused with storytelling, personality, and passion. Whether you’re commissioning a one-of-a-kind artwork, tuning into a live stream, or collecting original pieces, @omerglitch delivers a blend of creativity and craftsmanship that connects fans and creators alike.
